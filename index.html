How to play

Build the four foundations up by suit from Ace to King. In the columns, stack cards down in alternating colours; only a King may fill an empty column.

  • Touch / mouse: drag cards, or tap one to send it to its best spot. Tap the deck to draw.
  • Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Enter picks up and drops, Space draws, A auto-moves, H hint, Ctrl+Z undo, T themes.
  • Controller: D-pad moves, A pick up/drop, X auto-move, Y hint, B cancel/undo, LB undo, RB draw, Start menu, View themes.
